--- LINKEDIN / CLIENT-FACING ---
You cannot govern what you have never counted.

Most ServiceNow instances are inherited, not built — years of apps, plugins and
undocumented customizations nobody wrote down. SN Instance Scan walks the instance
application-by-application and produces the inventory: tables, business rules, script
includes, flows, integrations, ACLs, catalog items, cross-scope dependencies and
customizations on base-system tables.

One real scan: 5 apps, 84 tables profiled, 1,350 installed modules, 59 inbound
cross-references, 44 base-table customizations.

Six scan modes, one click, no configuration. Results land as queryable records plus a
downloadable PDF assessment report.

It is read-only by design — every query runs under the caller's own access, and the app
writes only to its own tables. Nothing in your instance is modified to produce the report.
